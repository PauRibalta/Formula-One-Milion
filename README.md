# F1 Simulator

Formula 1 Monte Carlo simulator written in Python.

## Features

- Race simulation
- Season simulation
- Driver ratings
- Car ratings
- Circuit modelling
- Monte Carlo analysis
- Driver mistakes during races
- Retirements and mechanical failures
- Weather simulation with wet/dry conditions
- Safety car and virtual safety car logic
- Team performance evolution across the season
- Historical calibration from 2024-2026 championship standings

Historical ratings use normalized championship points with these weights:

- Drivers: 2026 65%, 2025 25%, 2024 10%
- Teams: 2026 50%, 2025 30%, 2024 20%
- Cars: 2026 90%, 2025 7.5%, 2024 2.5%
